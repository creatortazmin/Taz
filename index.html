World Migratory Bird Day 🐦 October 10

Have you ever wondered how light pollution affects migratory birds?
Most songbirds rely on the stars and dark skies to navigate thousands of miles across the globe. But as our cities grow, bright artificial light at night (ALAN) spills into the sky, creating a glowing maze that masks the natural darkness. This constant urban glare throws off their internal clocks, disorienting night-flying species and turning dark skies into a dangerous journey.
